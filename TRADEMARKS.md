The gogol source code is licensed under the EUPL-1.2 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The license covers the code, not the name: as its Article 5 states, it grants no right to use the licensor's trade names or trademarks. "gogol" and its logos identify the official version of this project. This policy explains how you may use them, so that people always know which version they are trusting with their books.

## Allowed without asking

- Referring to the project by name, for example "based on gogol", "compatible with gogol" or an article about gogol.
- Redistributing unmodified official releases under the gogol name.
- Non-commercial community activity about the project, such as tutorials or meetups, as long as it is clear that it is not official.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Using the name or logos for a modified version. A fork needs a name that is not confusingly similar; it may say that it is based on gogol.
- Using the name or logos for a hosted service, product, company or domain name that could suggest it is official or endorsed.
- Using the name in any way that suggests the project endorses you or your product.
